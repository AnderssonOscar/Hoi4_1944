# 1944 - Downfall: Volkssturm call-ups in 1945 (see docs/CHANGELOG.md section 6)
#
# After the focus GER_form_volksturm (the first levy, autumn 1944), the
# Volkssturm was called out region by region as the front arrived. Each
# decision appears on the historical date and can be taken once the front
# has reached that region (an enemy holds part of one of its states, or a
# neighbouring state).
# Divisions are only raised in states Germany still holds (controls);
# create_unit never places them on enemy-held provinces.
#
#   decision                from        divisions (5 militia battalions each)
#   GER_volkssturm_east     1945.1.12   26  Soviet winter offensive from the Vistula, 12 January 1945
#   GER_volkssturm_oder     1945.1.31   10  Red Army reaches the Oder north of Kustrin, 31 January 1945
#   GER_volkssturm_west     1945.2.8     6  Western Allied offensive into the Rhineland (Veritable), 8 February 1945
#   GER_volkssturm_berlin   1945.4.16    5  Soviet attack from the Oder towards Berlin, 16 April 1945
#   total                               47  (+42 from the focus = 89 divisions, 445 battalions)
#
# 445 battalions of 1,000 men is about the 700+ real Volkssturm battalions
# (about 640 men each) that saw combat, most of them in the East (Hans Kissel).
# The units themselves are made in common/scripted_effects/GER_volkssturm_effects.txt.
###########################
